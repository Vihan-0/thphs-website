# Photography Plan & Media Strategy: Town Hall Public School

## Purpose

This document articulates the visual media strategy, section-by-section photo mapping allocations, photography guidelines, and a comprehensive future shoot checklist for Town Hall Public School.

---

## 1. Visual Media Strategy

Photography plays a pivotal role in conveying the warmth, academic focus, and community atmosphere of Town Hall Public School.

### Photography Principles
1. **Authenticity First**: Photos must capture genuine moments of student engagement, curiosity, and mentorship.
2. **Warmth & Natural Lighting**: Avoid harsh studio lighting or artificial posing.
3. **Inclusive Representation**: Show students across grade levels (Nursery through Class XII) involved in academic, sports, artistic, and collaborative activities.
4. **Clean Framing**: Emphasize uncluttered classroom backgrounds, well-maintained facilities, and safe campus grounds.

## 3. Future Photography Capture Checklist

The following shoot list details the specific shots required during the upcoming professional campus photography session to replace temporary placeholders:

### Exterior & Campus Infrastructure
- [ ] **Campus Exterior**: Wide shot of main school building entrance in Tilak Nagar, Aishbagh (Golden hour).
